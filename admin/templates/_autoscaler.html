Autoscaler

Load is absorbed cheapest first: replicas in seconds, nodes in about two minutes. Coming down the order reverses — shed replicas first, remove the node later. The master is not a worker: it is the control plane, and while no worker exists it carries your components itself and nothing is billed. Capacity is measured from real CPU and memory, never configured, and each component's scaling policy travels with it as labels on its own service.
